# What goes here

Your evidence images. Three are required, one per face of a card:

- `dashboard.png` — the normal view, with live data loaded
- `loading.png` — the loading face, captured with the network throttled
- `error.png` — the error face, captured after you broke the request on purpose

Building more than one card? Add its three with the card's name in front, like
`weather-loading.png`. Anything is fine as long as a grader can tell what they are looking at —
`Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 11.42.07 PM.png` is not.

Leave this file in place. It is what keeps the folder in your repo before you have added any images.
